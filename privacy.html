Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is handled in connection with the Calculator+ mobile application (the "App"). The App is published by Junzhao Liu ("we", "us" or "our"). By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Data controller and contact

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and similar laws, the data controller for any personal data we process is Junzhao Liu. You may contact us about this Privacy Policy or your personal data at appdevsht@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The App has no user accounts, and we operate no servers that receive data from the App.
  • We do not receive, store or have access to the photos, videos, files or notes you place in the App ("Vault Content"), or to your secret code, recovery key or backup password.
  • The App displays advertising provided by Google. Google collects certain data through its software, as described in Section 5.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Vault Content

Vault Content is encrypted on your device using keys protected by your device's Secure Enclave, where available, and your secret code. Vault Content is processed only on your device and, if you choose, in the services described below. We cannot access, recover or decrypt it.

  • iCloud Sync (optional, off by default). If you enable iCloud Sync, the App stores your encrypted Vault Content in your private iCloud database, and an encrypted copy of your vault key in your iCloud Keychain. Both are provided by Apple Inc. under your agreement with Apple and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to your iCloud account or its contents.
  • Recovery key (optional). A recovery key is generated and displayed only on your device. We never receive it.
  • Encrypted Backup (optional). A backup is a password-protected file saved to a location you choose. We never receive it.

4. Device permissions

The App imports media through Apple's system photo picker, which does not grant the App access to your photo library. The App requests photo library access only if you ask it to delete originals from your library after importing them, or to delete a screenshot you select in the system photo picker after taking it while the vault is open. With limited access, only items you have shared with the App can be deleted. iOS asks you to confirm every deletion. The App requests add-only access only when you save a copy to your photo library. You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings. No data from your photo library is transmitted to us.

5. Advertising

The App is free of charge and supported by advertising served by Google LLC and its affiliates ("Google") through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Advertisements appear only within the vault. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects through its SDK.

Data collected by Google. According to Google's data disclosure and the privacy manifests of the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent SDK, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers other than the advertising identifier (IDFA), such as an app-scoped identifier;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • product interaction and advertising data, such as ad views and taps; and
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs, performance metrics and other diagnostic information.

Google may link device identifiers, approximate location, product interaction and advertising data to your device. Google does not link diagnostic data to you. Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics, for its own advertising and marketing, and to show the consent form where required.

Non-personalized ads. The App requests non-personalized advertising only and does not request permission to track you under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. As a result, the App cannot access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), the IDFA is not shared with Google, and ads are not selected based on your activity in other apps or websites.

Consent. Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is presented within the App before information is stored on, or accessed from, your device for advertising purposes. You may decline. If you decline, the App remains fully functional and Google may serve limited ads. You can review or withdraw your choices at any time in the App under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices, where available.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Data we collect

We do not collect personal data through the App. The App contains no analytics or crash reporting of our own. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, de-identified crash and usage reports in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message, and will use them only to respond to you. We retain such correspondence only as long as necessary to resolve your request.

7. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personal data is processed on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)), for the storage of and access to information on your device for advertising purposes, where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), in funding the App through advertising and responding to your enquiries, where consent is not required.

8. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on legally recognized transfer mechanisms, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, for transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any personal data collected through the App. Vault Content remains under your control:

  • Settings → Erase Vault deletes the vault from your device and, if you choose, from iCloud.
  • Settings → iCloud Sync → Delete Vault from iCloud deletes the iCloud copy of the vault and the iCloud Keychain copy of its key.
  • Deleting the App deletes the vault stored on that device.

Data collected by Google is retained in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

10. Security

Vault Content is protected with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, with keys derived from your secret code and bound to your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Your secret code cannot be recovered by us. If you lose your code without a recovery key or backup, your Vault Content cannot be recovered.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you and receive a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate personal data corrected;
  • have your personal data deleted;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your personal data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not hold personal data collected through the App, most requests can be fulfilled directly within the App as described in Section 9, or with Google regarding advertising data. You may also contact us at appdevsht@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

12. United States residents

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, and similar U.S. state laws. In the last 12 months, the only categories of personal information collected in connection with the App are those collected by Google as described in Section 5, for the purpose of serving and measuring advertising. Where offered, you can exercise additional choices under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

13. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, or the higher minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data in connection with the App, please contact us.

14. Disclosure to authorities

We do not possess Vault Content, secret codes, recovery keys or encryption keys, and therefore cannot provide them to any third party, including law enforcement or government authorities.
