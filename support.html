Support

Questions or problems? Email appdevsht@gmail.com. Please never send your secret code, recovery key or backup password.

Opening and closing the vault

On the calculator, type your secret code and press =. The vault locks as soon as you leave the app. You can also lock it with Settings → Lock Now.

The calculator won't accept my code

After 10 wrong codes, the app pauses all codes for 30 seconds, and the pause doubles with each further wrong code, up to 64 minutes. Wait for the pause to end, then try again. Changing the clock doesn't shorten it.

I forgot my code

Nobody can recover your code, including us. You can get your items back only if you set up one of these before you forgot it:

  • iCloud Sync with a recovery key: delete and reinstall the app, tap Restore from iCloud, tap Use Recovery Key Instead, enter your recovery key, then choose a new code.
  • Encrypted Backup: delete and reinstall the app, set up a new code, then open the vault and tap Settings → Restore from Backup and enter the backup's password.

Deleting the app erases the vault on that device. Do it only if you have one of the above.

Moving to a new iPhone or iPad

Moving your apps to a new device doesn't carry the vault's device-bound key, so use one of these:

  • iCloud Sync: on the new device, sign in to the same Apple Account, install the app, tap Restore from iCloud and enter your code.
  • Encrypted Backup: save a backup on the old device, then on the new one tap Settings → Restore from Backup inside the vault.

iCloud Sync

  • Sync runs only while the vault is open. Open the vault on each device to bring it up to date.
  • Synced data counts against your own iCloud storage. If it's full, nothing is deleted. Free up space or upgrade your storage.
  • Turning sync off keeps the iCloud copy. To remove it, use Settings → iCloud Sync → Delete Vault from iCloud.

Recently deleted items

Deleted items stay in Settings → Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can restore them.

Ads and privacy choices

Ads appear only inside the vault. If ad consent rules apply where you live, change your choices in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.
