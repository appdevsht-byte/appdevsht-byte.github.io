Calculator+

A working calculator with an encrypted vault inside.

Calculator+ works as an ordinary calculator. Type your secret code and press = to open a private vault for photos, videos, files and notes. Leave the app and the vault locks.

  • Everything in the vault is encrypted on your device.
  • Optional iCloud Sync stores only encrypted data in your own private iCloud.
  • No account, and no servers of ours.
  • Free, with ads shown only inside the vault, never on the calculator.

Runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac computers with Apple silicon.
